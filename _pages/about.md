## About Me

Hi, I am **Aaron Hwang**. My research and development focus on algorithm design and software development for metabolomics and proteomics spectral analysis, as well as multi-omics data integration (covering bulk and single-cell metabolomics, proteomics, and transcriptomics).

My primary goal is to help streamline mass spectrometry workflows and extract biological insights from complex biological datasets.

*   **Primary Focus:** Designing spectral analysis workflows and integrating multi-omics data to help uncover underlying biological patterns.
*   **Data Visualization:** To complement data pipelines, I also enjoy developing publication-ready, interactive visualizations using R to help present scientific findings clearly and effectively.

---

## Current & Future Work

I am currently working on next-generation mass spectrometry (MS) analysis tools for chemistry and biology, aiming to build automated and user-friendly analytical pipelines. I am also open to developing custom algorithms tailored to specific spectral or multi-omics datasets.
